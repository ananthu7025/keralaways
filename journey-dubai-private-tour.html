Private Itinerary

Your 4-Day Schedule

Seamless private transport, licensed guides, and curated Arabian experiences.

Day 1

Arrival & Dhow Cruise Dinner

↓

Airport Arrival • Evening Cruise (7:00 PM – 11:00 PM)

Private airport pickup upon arrival at Dubai International Airport (DXB) and comfortable transfer to your hotel. Settle in and relax before your evening excursion.

  • Private Hotel Pickup: Approximately 7:00–7:30 PM (pickup time may vary slightly by season and sunset).
  • Dubai Creek Dhow Cruise: Cruising approximately 8:30–10:30 PM aboard a traditional illuminated wooden dhow.
  • Buffet Dinner & Shows: Indulge in an international buffet dinner accompanied by live cultural music and entertainment.
  • Private Hotel Return: Expected drop-off at your hotel around 11:00 PM.

Overnight: Ibis Styles or similar 3-star Dubai hotel

Day 2

Half-Day Private Dubai Sightseeing

↓

9:00 AM Pickup • Return Around 1:00–1:30 PM

Following breakfast at the hotel, embark on a private morning journey through historic Old Dubai and modern icons with a licensed Dubai driver-cum-guide.

  • Heritage & Souqs: Traditional Abra boat crossing across Dubai Creek; explore the shimmering Gold Souq and fragrant Spice Souq.
  • Landmark Photo Stops: Dubai Frame, the architectural wonder Museum of the Future, Burj Khalifa, and the iconic Burj Al Arab.
  • Coastal & Island Drive: Stroll through the charming canals of Madinat Jumeirah and enjoy a scenic drive through the world-famous Palm Jumeirah.
  • Afternoon at Leisure: Drop-off back at your hotel around 1:00–1:30 PM for shopping or relaxation at your own pace.

Overnight: Ibis Styles or similar 3-star Dubai hotel

Day 3

Desert Safari & BBQ Dinner

↓

Pickup Around 2:00–2:15 PM • Return Around 9:30 PM

Spend a relaxed morning at your hotel. In the afternoon, your 4x4 desert safari adventure begins as you head toward the magnificent red sand dunes of the Dubai desert.

  • Thrilling Dune Bashing: Exhilarating 4x4 dune drive over undulating sand dunes with experienced safari drivers.
  • Sunset & Activities: Scenic sunset photo stop in the desert, sandboarding down towering dunes, and a short camel ride experience.
  • Desert Camp Welcome: Refresh with unlimited soft drinks, Arabic coffee, dates, savory starters, and complimentary small henna tattoo application.
  • BBQ Dinner & Live Shows: Savor a sumptuous barbecue buffet dinner under starlit skies accompanied by live Belly dance, traditional Tanoura spinning dance, and thrilling fire shows.
  • Hotel Return: Return transfer to your hotel around 9:30 PM.

Overnight: Ibis Styles or similar 3-star Dubai hotel

Day 4

Miracle Garden & Airport Departure

↓

Hotel Checkout • Garden Visit • Airport Drop-Off

Enjoy breakfast at the hotel and check out with all luggage. Your private chauffeur will take you on your final enchanting Dubai experience.

  • Dubai Miracle Garden: Proceed to the world's largest natural flower garden featuring over 150 million blooming flowers crafted into fantastical sculptures and planes.
  • Exploration Time: Allow approximately 2–3 hours to wander along flower-lined paths and capture memorable photographs.
  • Private Airport Transfer: Direct transfer to Dubai International Airport (DXB) in good time for your return flight departure.

* Note: Dubai Miracle Garden is subject to seasonal opening dates (usually November to April). Departure timing is adjusted according to your flight schedule.
